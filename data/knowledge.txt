Source: Bhagavad Gita — principle of focusing on action rather than attachment to results.
Teaching summary: One should focus sincerely on one's duty and actions without becoming consumed by the outcome. This can help when anxiety about success or failure becomes overwhelming.
---
Source: Bhagavad Gita — principle of equanimity.
Teaching summary: A balanced mind is encouraged in success and failure, helping a person act with steadiness rather than being controlled by constantly changing results.
---
Source: Bhagavad Gita — principle of disciplined action.
Teaching summary: Growth comes through disciplined effort, self-control and purposeful action rather than simply wishing for a desired outcome.
---
Source: Upanishadic wisdom — self-inquiry.
Teaching summary: Reflection on the nature of the self, awareness and one's deeper values can help a person look beyond temporary circumstances and develop clarity.
